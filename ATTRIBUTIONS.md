## Photographs

Part V compares generated images against real photographs collected from
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org). Each photograph on the page
carries its own credit: click any photograph in Part V to show the
photographer's name, the licence it was released under, and a link to its
Commons page. Licences are as stated by the uploader, predominantly CC BY-SA
and CC BY.

## Generated images

All other images on this site were generated for this study by the seven
text-to-image models it examines. They depict nothing real and no real person.
