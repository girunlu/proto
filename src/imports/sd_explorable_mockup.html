Interactive Explorable · SD 2.1 · XAI

What does Stable Diffusion
assume before you say anything?

Stable Diffusion does not generate from a neutral starting point. Every prompt arrives into a model that has already made decisions — about who a person looks like, which culture is the default, and what "realistic" means. This explorable makes those decisions visible.
